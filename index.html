01 / About

A mobile engineer who finishes the job.

I'm a Flutter engineer based in Tashkent, Uzbekistan. My story started with a handful of freelance gigs, taken on out of pure curiosity. Over the six years since, that curiosity has carried me through a Tech Lead role at one of Tashkent's first delivery platforms, and into shipping apps across delivery, fintech, streaming, marketplace and crypto.

What I love most about this work isn't just shipping a v1 — it's keeping it alive. Stability, release cycles and analytics are the parts that decide whether an app actually lasts, and they get just as much of my attention as the build itself.

Most recently I was at EMCD, a crypto / Web3 platform with 35K+ monthly active users, where I led two end-to-end refactors of the Coinhold wallet and moved the team onto AI-assisted testing. Open to Flutter and mobile lead roles — remote-first, worldwide. Based in Tashkent (UTC+5): full overlap with Gulf hours and most of the European workday.
