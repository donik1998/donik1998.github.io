01 / About

A mobile engineer who finishes the job.

I'm a Flutter engineer based in Tashkent, Uzbekistan. My story started with a handful of freelance gigs, taken on out of pure curiosity. Over the five years since, that curiosity has carried me through a Tech Lead role at one of Tashkent's first delivery platforms, and into shipping apps across delivery, fintech, streaming, marketplace and crypto.
