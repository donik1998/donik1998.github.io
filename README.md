## Highlights

- Six years, nine products in production across banking, delivery, fintech, streaming, marketplace and crypto
- **Hamkorbank** (now) — mobile core team of a retail banking app used by 1.9 million people a day; the team also works on the app's protection (secure storage, SSL pinning, biometrics, root/jailbreak checks)
- **Express24** — inherited a B2B merchant platform at 68% crash-free users and reached 98% in two months; replaced order polling with a real-time WebSocket pipeline; migrated the codebase to Feature-First DDD; promoted to mobile tech lead in eight months and led three engineers
- **EMCD** — led two end-to-end refactors of the wallet app (35,000+ monthly active users), both delivered on schedule; took Coinhold (about 11,000 daily users holding $60M+) past 90% test coverage, moved the team onto AI-assisted test writing, and built earnings sharing with PNG encoding in a separate isolate
- **GameFit** — co-founder and engineer of a platform for gaming clubs: a Flutter app, a Spring Boot backend, a Next.js CRM and a .NET agent for club PCs
- **Contract work** — built Million TV from zero, shipped the 7Tech marketplace to web, iOS and Android from one codebase, and delivered the two-surface Cuppy platform

## Tech

Hand-written HTML and CSS — no frameworks, no build step, zero dependencies, and no webfonts: the Swiss Editorial type is the system Helvetica stack, so the pages make no external requests at all. Trilingual (EN/RU/UZ), responsive, light and dark, respects `prefers-reduced-motion`, print-optimised CV pages — one CV per language, each a single A4 page.
